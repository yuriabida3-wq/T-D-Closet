How to get it

Delivery & pickup

Nationwide delivery

We ship countrywide via courier or bus parcel. Choose your zone at checkout, the exact fee is shown before you pay.

  • Delivery in 1 to 3 days
  • Fee confirmed at checkout
  • Pay online or on pickup

In-store pickup

Come to Horizon Plaza, 4th Floor, Shop 1011. Try before you buy. Take your time, no pressure.

  • Open Mon to Sat, 8 AM to 8 PM
  • Opposite the bus station
  • No delivery fee
